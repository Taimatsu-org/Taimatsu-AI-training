Working with Claude

A short training for every team at Taimatsu. No AI experience needed.

What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant made by Anthropic. You write a message in normal language, and Claude writes back. It can read files you give it, write and edit text, translate, summarise, analyse data, and help with code.

Think of it as a very fast, very well-read new colleague. It is helpful, but it does not know our company, and it can make mistakes. You are still responsible for the result.

Good at

  • First drafts of emails, documents and slides
  • Summarising long documents
  • Translating between Japanese and English
  • Explaining things step by step
  • Turning messy notes into tables or lists
  • Brainstorming ideas

Be careful with

  • Facts, numbers, dates and laws: it can be confidently wrong
  • Anything after its training date, unless it searches the web
  • Our internal rules and context: it only knows what you tell it
  • Final decisions: a person must check and decide

The 3 golden rules

  1. Check your account. If you have a Taimatsu Claude account, use the Taimatsu organization when sharing work information.
  2. Protect sensitive information. Keep passwords and API keys out of AI, and take care with personal and confidential information. See what to consider before sharing.
  3. Check before you use it. Read every answer before you send, publish or act on it.
